Say the decision in the title.

Open with the verdict. The reader knows the recommendation before the first scroll; everything after is support.

Recommendation. Which option, the one reason that carries it, and what would change your mind.

Grammar

  • Verdict-first title. The title states the decision, display voice, accent full stop.
  • Prose sits at 65ch. The measure token (--container-prose) is the only line length.
  • Status-bearing lists are tables. Items carrying owner, status, or what-it-unlocks render as columns, never bullets.
  • Headers divide; nothing sits on top of one. No eyebrow above content.
  • Footnotes are quiet sentence-case body. Never uppercase tracked-out mono.
  • Money, flow, and sequence each earn a drawn figure. A long doc with no figures is under-visualized.

Print + PDF

  • Print expands everything. Progressive disclosure opens on beforeprint; the paper copy is complete.
  • Light theme on paper. PDFs export from the light flip block; dark ink floods are a deliberate cover choice only.
  • The badge closes the footer on named engagements; white-label drops it.
  • Template: the starter kit's templates/doc.html is the working skeleton.
