Rules

  • Animate transform and opacity only. Never blur, filter, gradient stops, or layout properties.
  • Reduced-motion is honored everywhere. Every animation ships a functional non-animated equivalent.
  • Motion shows behavior, never decorates. A convection current earns animation; a heading does not.
  • One orchestrated moment beats scattered effects. Spend --dur-4 once, keep everything around it quiet.
  • Durations come from the tokens. A literal ms value in a surface is a smell the way a literal hex is.
