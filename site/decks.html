Stage rules

  • Dark stage by default. The tokens' root theme is the projector theme.
  • One claim per slide. The action title IS the slide; everything else supports it.
  • Display voice. Heavy weight, tight tracking, short statement, accent full stop.
  • Mono kickers only. The kicker is a machine label; the claim is for humans.
  • Slide-deck emptiness. A deck breathes harder than a page; when in doubt, cut the third support.
  • Numbers are evidence. Tabular numerals, one per claim where possible, provenance available on ask.
