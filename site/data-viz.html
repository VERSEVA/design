Doctrine

Synthesized from the portfolio's shipped viz (Spectre, the NEMIXO console, the UFITRA reference mockups) and held to the dataviz six checks.

RuleIt looks like
numbers on marksBars carry their count; hover is backup, never required
axes are a luxuryNo gridline forests; one hairline axis or a dashed reference line at most
magnitude = one hue--data-0..3, the accent ladder; intensity, never category
two series max--data-3 then --data-alt (+ dash); a third folds to small multiples
denominator first"14 of 31 decided", never a naked 45%
meaning colors deltasA falling 5K time renders green
identity never color-aloneWord + glyph ride along; sr data behind every chart
takeaway captionsLabel the meaning, not the axis

Anti-patterns

If a chart matches one of these, it is wrong.

NeverA dual-axis chart. Two measures of different scale get two charts or an index.
NeverA gridline forest. Numbers on the marks; one reference line at most.
NeverA rainbow. Magnitude is one hue; a third series folds to small multiples.
NeverColor-alone identity. A word or glyph always rides along.
NeverA naked percentage. Carry the denominator: "14 of 31 decided".
NeverAn axis caption as the takeaway. Label the meaning.
